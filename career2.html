Job Description

We are looking for an experienced Physical Therapist to join our Rehabilitation Services team. This role involves assessing patients' physical conditions, developing personalized treatment plans, and guiding patients through rehabilitation exercises. The successful candidate will demonstrate expertise in physical therapy and a commitment to helping patients achieve their recovery goals.

Responsibilities:

  • Licensed Physical Therapist
  • Doctorate or Master’s degree in Physical Therapy
  • At least 3 years of clinical experience
  • Proficiency in creating treatment plans
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team

Perks & Benefits:

  • Competitive salary with performance incentives
  • Health insurance coverage (medical, dental, vision)
  • Paid time off and holiday pay
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Flexible work hours
  • Access to modern rehabilitation equipment
